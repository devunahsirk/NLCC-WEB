About Us

Learn more about our lab's mission, vision, and see our researchers in action.

The Nara Lab on Computational Cognition (NLCC), based within the School of Biosciences and Bioengineering at IIT Mandi, is a young research group investigating how the brain interprets the world and constructs its representation of it. Our work focuses on the brain's remarkable ability to anticipate and predict what comes next, and how this predictive machinery supports perception, language, and behaviour.

We combine brain imaging (EEG, MEG, fMRI), computational modelling, and artificial intelligence to study how neural activity unfolds across space and time. By comparing biological brains with artificial networks, we aim to uncover shared rules of computation that explain how the brain suppresses the expected, highlights the surprising, and transforms experience into behaviour.

Current projects in the lab range from studying how emotions are recognised in neurotypical individuals to speech decoding, testing theories of predictive coding, and building brain–computer interfaces that translate neural signals into movement and interaction. In parallel, we develop AI-based tools to investigate brain–behaviour relationships, with applications in neurodevelopmental conditions.

By blending cognitive neuroscience, biomedical engineering, neurolinguistics, and machine learning, our goal is to bridge the gap between biology and computation, advancing both the fundamental understanding of the mind and practical innovations in health and technology.
