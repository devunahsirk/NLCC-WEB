Join us

We welcome motivated candidates from interdisciplinary academic backgrounds who are interested in our ongoing and future projects.

Ph.D. Program

The lab is affiliated with the School of Biosciences and Bioengineering (SBB), the Centre for Human-Computer Interaction (CHCi), and the Indian Knowledge System and Mental Health Applications Centre (IKSMHA).

Preference is given to candidates who are supported by external fellowships such as DST INSPIRE, CSIR-UGC NET, UGC NET, ICMR, and similar schemes.

We host Joint Ph.D. students through five partner institutions affiliated with IIT Mandi:

  • IIT Jammu
  • IIT Ropar
  • SVNIT Surat
  • Punjab Engineering College (PEC)
  • Gurugram University

At present, the lab is not accepting new Ph.D. students.

Internships

Internship positions are limited and are offered based on current research requirements.

Interested applicants should review the research profiles of our Ph.D. scholars and contact those whose interests align with their own.

Please note that the lab does not host internships undertaken solely to fulfil mandatory degree requirements.
