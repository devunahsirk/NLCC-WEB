Our Research Focus

We bridge the gap between biology and computation by blending cognitive neuroscience, biomedical engineering, neurolinguistics and machine learning. Advancing both the fundamental understanding of the mind ad practical innovations in health technology .

Cognitive Modeling

Building mathematical and algorithmic models that accurately simulate diverse human cognitive processes spanning from memory to complex decision making.

Machine Learning

Leveraging biologically-inspired neural networks and reinforcement learning to develop more robust, interpretable, and adaptable artificial intelligence.

Human-AI Interaction

Investigating how artificial cognitive architectures can be designed to better align with human reasoning patterns for seamless collaboration.
