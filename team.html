Meet Our Team

The interdisciplinary minds behind the Nara Lab on Computational Cognition.

DR
Principal Investigator

Dr. Sanjeev Nara

Dr Sanjeev Nara is an Assistant Professor at IIT Mandi, specialising in biomedical signal/image processing, as well as cognitive neuroscience. His academic background includes degrees in Biomedical Engineering and a Ph.D. in Cognitive Neuroscience from BCBL, Spain. Before joining IIT Mandi, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow and Data Scientist at the University of Giessen, Germany, where he studied neuroaesthetics and vision using deep neural networks. He leads the Nara Lab on Computational Cognition, focusing on perception, cognition, and their computational modeling.
